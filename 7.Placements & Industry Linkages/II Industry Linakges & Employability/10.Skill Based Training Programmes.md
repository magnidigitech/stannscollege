**Skill-Based Training Programmes**

St. Ann\'s College for Women conducts skill-based training programmes to
enhance students' employability, professional competencies, and career
readiness. These programmes focus on developing communication skills,
leadership qualities, digital literacy, and workplace preparedness.

**Training Areas**

• Soft skills and personality development\
• Communication and presentation skills\
• Aptitude and reasoning training\
• Leadership and teamwork\
• Digital and computer skills\
• Entrepreneurship and innovation\
• Interview and group discussion skills

The institution regularly organizes training sessions, workshops, and
career-oriented programmes aligned with industry expectations and
emerging employment trends to prepare students for professional success.
