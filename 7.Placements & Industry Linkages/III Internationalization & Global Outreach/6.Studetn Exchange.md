**6. Student Exchange / Faculty Exchange**

St. Ann\'s College for Women encourages academic interaction and
exchange opportunities through collaborative programmes, online learning
platforms, and institutional partnerships.

**Objectives**

• Promote academic and cultural exchange\
• Enhance global learning experiences\
• Encourage collaborative teaching and learning\
• Improve communication and intercultural competencies

The institution supports opportunities that broaden students' and
faculty members' global perspectives and academic exposure.
