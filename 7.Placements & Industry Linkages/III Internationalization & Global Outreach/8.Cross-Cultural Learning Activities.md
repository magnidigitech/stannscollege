**8. Cross-Cultural Learning Activities**

St. Ann\'s College for Women promotes cross-cultural learning activities
to encourage mutual respect, cultural understanding, and global
awareness among students.

**Activities**

• Cultural exchange programmes\
• International days and celebrations\
• Language and communication activities\
• Awareness programmes on global cultures and values\
• Collaborative learning and interaction sessions

Cross-cultural learning activities help students develop inclusiveness,
communication skills, and global citizenship values.
