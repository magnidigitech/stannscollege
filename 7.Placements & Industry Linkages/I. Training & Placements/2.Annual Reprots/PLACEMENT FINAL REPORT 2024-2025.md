**ABOUT THE PLACEMENT CELL**

**Placement Report for the Academic Year: 2024-25**

**Report Prepared By : Dr. J. Pratapa Reddy, TPO.**

The Placement Cell of St. Ann's College for Women plays a pivotal role
in bridging the gap between students and the corporate world. The cell
is committed to providing students with career opportunities that align
with their skills, qualifications, and aspirations. This report
summarizes the placement activities for the academic year 2024-25.

Placement Cell equips the students with all the skills they needed to be
placed in their dream companies, without knocking on various doors for
jobs and enhances the reputation of the Institution with its placement
records .It creates awareness about career planning and career mapping
and organizes various training programs to train the students in the
areas of Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Verbal Reasoning,
Soft Skills and Interview Skills through reputed training organizations.

**OBJECTIVES & STYLE OF FUNCTIONING:-**

**OBJECTIVES:-**

- Inform to the students about various career opportunities and higher
  studies.

- To Organized campus Interviews for offering placement services to
  Students.

- To Established MOU with various Government, Industries Professional,
  Private Placement agencies etc.

- The cell conduct programmes on Aptitude, Soft skills and Communication
  skills for students.

- To assist our students in obtaining final placement in reputed
  companies.

- To build confidence among students.

**FUNCTIONING:-**

- The Placement Cell has a team of students which manages the
  operations.

- Everything is done professionally, formally and transparent; under the
  guidance of the placement guide, Dr. Sr. Fatima Rani. P, Principal and
  the placement officer.
