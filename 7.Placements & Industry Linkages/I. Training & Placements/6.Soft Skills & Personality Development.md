**Soft Skills & Personality Development**

The college gives special emphasis to personality development and soft
skills training to improve students' confidence and professionalism.

**Focus Areas**

- Communication & Presentation Skills

- Public Speaking

- Interview Skills

- Resume Writing

- Leadership Development

- Interpersonal Skills

- Professional Etiquette

- Self-Confidence Building

These programmes help students become career-ready and socially
responsible individuals.
