**Internships & Industry Exposure**

Experiential learning is encouraged through internships, industrial
visits, field work, and industry interaction programmes.

**Internship Opportunities**

- Industry-Based Internships

- NGO Internships

- Project Internships

- Summer Training Programmes

- Community Engagement Activities

**Industry Exposure Activities**

- Industrial Visits

- Field Trips

- Guest Lectures by Industry Experts

- Industry--Academia Interaction Sessions

- Live Projects and Case Studies

Such exposure helps students connect classroom learning with real-world
applications.
