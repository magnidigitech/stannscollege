# Code of Conduct for Employees

## Overview

The Code of Conduct for employees of St. Ann’s College for Women is framed in accordance with the **Manual of Procedures of Higher Education (2021)** issued by The Society of St. Anne. 

This code defines the **professional standards, ethical values, and responsibilities** expected from all teaching and non-teaching staff, ensuring a disciplined, respectful, and academically enriching environment.

---

## Purpose

* To uphold **integrity, professionalism, and institutional values**
* To ensure **ethical behavior and accountability**
* To promote a **safe, respectful, and disciplined workplace**
* To maintain the **reputation and academic standards** of the institution

---

## Scope

This policy applies to:

* All **teaching staff**
* All **non-teaching staff**
* All employees irrespective of designation or appointment type

---

# General Rules & Responsibilities

## Identity & Administration

* Staff must **wear ID cards at all times**
* ID must be returned upon resignation
* Address changes must be reported within **3 days**

---

## Discipline & Professional Conduct

* Maintain **integrity, punctuality, and dedication**
* Follow all **institutional rules and lawful instructions**
* Avoid **misconduct, indiscipline, and disruptive behavior**
* No **smoking or substance use** on campus
* Maintain **cordial relationships with colleagues and students**
* No **private tuition without permission**
* No **unauthorized sharing of institutional information**

---

## Workplace Responsibilities

* Report to duty on time and **record attendance properly**
* Do not leave workplace without permission
* Follow **“No Work – No Pay”** rule for unauthorized absence
* Ensure **proper use of institutional property**

---

## Professional Ethics

* Maintain **fairness in student assessment**
* Avoid **bias, malpractice, or misconduct**
* Uphold dignity of the **teaching profession**

---

## Public Behaviour

* Maintain **professional conduct in public and campus interactions**
* Avoid **violence, discrimination, or abusive behavior**
* No **political or union-related activities within campus**

---

# Employment Rules

## Working Hours

* Standard: **8 hours per day**
* May vary based on institutional needs
* Additional duties may be assigned

---

## Employment Restrictions

* No **external employment without approval**
* No **business or trade activities on campus**
* No **collection of money from students without authorization**

---

## Safety Responsibilities

* Report hazards, risks, or accidents immediately
* Follow safety guidelines and precautions

---

# Leave & Vacation Policy

## General Leave Rules

* Leave must be **applied in advance**
* Subject to **institutional requirements**
* Leave is **not a right**
* Unauthorized absence may lead to disciplinary action

---

## Casual Leave (CL)

* 15 days per year
* Maximum 3 days at a time
* Cannot be carried forward

---

## Medical Leave

* Up to 5 days (extendable to 10 days)
* Requires medical certificate if more than 3 days

---

## Maternity Leave

* 3 months with pay (conditions apply)

---

## Leave Without Pay

* Up to 10 days under special conditions

---

## Official Leave

* Granted for academic duties, training, or institutional work

---

## Vacation

* Teaching staff: Minimum 45 days
* Non-teaching staff: Minimum 30 days
* Subject to institutional requirements

---

# Disciplinary Framework

## Misconduct Includes

* Absenteeism, negligence, or disobedience
* Misuse of institutional resources
* Harassment or unethical behavior
* Disclosure of confidential information
* Substance abuse or violence
* Corruption or bribery

---

## Disciplinary Authorities

* Principal → Minor penalties
* Governing Body → Major decisions
* Superior General → Appellate authority

---

## Minor Penalties

* Warning / Censure
* Fine
* Suspension (up to 7 days)
* Withholding increments or promotion

---

## Major Penalties

* Reduction in rank/pay
* Compulsory retirement
* Termination or dismissal

---

## Disciplinary Process

* Written notice and explanation
* Opportunity for hearing
* Enquiry process for major cases
* Right to appeal

---

## Suspension Rules

* Applicable during enquiry or serious misconduct
* 50% salary (subsistence allowance) initially
* Restrictions on campus entry and duties

---

# Separation & Retirement

## Resignation

* Notice or salary in lieu
* Proper handover required
* Subject to approval

---

## Termination

* Based on misconduct or institutional requirements
* As per legal and institutional norms

---

## Superannuation

* Retirement age: **58 years**
* Voluntary retirement possible under conditions

---

## Property Handover

* All institutional property must be returned
* Damages may be recovered

---

# Institutional Values

* Professionalism & Integrity
* Transparency & Accountability
* Respect & Inclusivity
* Commitment to Excellence

---

# Conclusion

This Code of Conduct ensures a **structured, ethical, and disciplined work environment**, enabling staff to contribute effectively to the institution’s mission of delivering **quality education and holistic development**.
